Cedalion

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026.

Who uses Cedalion

Cedalion is a personal app used only by its developer and owner (Rodrigo-Aparisi). It has no other users and collects no data from anyone else.

Google data it accesses

  • Gmail messages, labels and drafts in the owner's account (Gmail scope gmail.modify).
  • Events on the owner's Google Calendar (scope calendar.events).
  • Calendars that Cedalion itself creates, and their events (scope calendar.app.created).

How it is used

Only to provide the owner's own features: email digests and sorting, draft replies that the owner approves, the owner's schedule, and the owner's study plan.

Where it is kept

On the owner's own computer and the owner's own backup at home. The developer runs no server that stores it.

Sharing

Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with any other person. To answer the owner's own requests, relevant parts may be processed by Anthropic's Claude AI service under the owner's own account, and short notices are sent to the owner's private Telegram chat. Nothing else receives it.

Cedalion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Cedalion's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting Cedalion's folders on the owner's computer deletes the data it kept.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy is published on this page. Contact: Rodrigo-Aparisi on GitHub.
