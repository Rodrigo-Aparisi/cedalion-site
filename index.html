Cedalion

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer.

Cedalion is a private project. It is built and used by one person, its developer (Rodrigo-Aparisi), and it is not offered to anyone else.

What it does with Google

With the owner's permission, Cedalion connects to the owner's own Google account to:

  • read and sort the owner's Gmail, write short digests, and prepare draft replies; it sends or deletes a message only when the owner asks it to;
  • read and manage the owner's Google Calendar, including a separate study calendar that Cedalion creates for the owner's study plan.
